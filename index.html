Services

What I do

Clean, reliable data

Combine data from different systems, profile it, fix errors and duplicates, and check every figure against its source before it reaches a report.

Dashboards and reports

Build Power BI dashboards and Excel reports that answer the questions managers actually ask, and explain the findings in plain language.

Automation

Write Python and SQL that replaces repetitive manual work, such as pulling figures out of PDFs or reconciling records, so teams spend time on decisions instead.

Analysis and statistics

Regression, correlation, design of experiments and predictive models, from academic research through to survey and performance data.

Working with stakeholders

Work out what each report needs to answer, who reads it and how often, then deliver it on time to management and external sponsors.

Currently exploring

Machine learning, natural language processing and semantic search, applied to large collections of documents such as South African water sector reports.
